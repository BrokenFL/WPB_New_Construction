# News Source Policy

WPB New Construction displays external development headlines as source-linked records, not as original reporting.

- Use original headlines, original source names, publication dates, short neutral descriptions, and direct article links.
- Do not scrape full article text or copy paywalled article bodies.
- Prefer public/free sources: City of West Palm Beach, WPB CRA, WPTV, WPBF, CBS12, WFLX, Florida YIMBY, accessible development publications, and official project or project-sponsor releases.
- Press releases are allowed only when clearly treated as press-release or project-sponsor material.
- Exclude or deprioritize hard paywalls, login-only links, duplicate syndication, generic lifestyle content, and development-unrelated stories.
- Raw RSS/search candidates default to `needs-review`; only `published` items render on the public site.
- When a story is confidently tied to a building, image resolution must prefer that exact building's media before corridor imagery.
