- **Maison d’Or:** The building address remains **South Flagler Drive, West Palm Beach, FL**, without a street number while 3705 and 3773 conflict. The verified **3014 S Dixie Highway** sales gallery is a separate field. [Official project site](https://livemaisondor.com/) and [gallery page](https://livemaisondor.com/the-gallery/) support the sales path and published starting guidance from $5.7 million; [official residences and plans](https://livemaisondor.com/the-residences/) support released layouts. - **Alba Palm Beach:** The [developer’s June 2026 completion report](https://www.albapalmbeach.com/press/bgi-capital-and-blue-road-reach-completion-of-alba-palm-beach-in-west-palm-beach) supports a completed construction stage. The [current residences page](https://www.albapalmbeach.com/residences/) advertises starting guidance just under $3 million; the older $2.5 million press figure is retained as historical context, not current public guidance. [ONE Sotheby’s development page](https://www.onesothebysrealty.com/our-developments/alba-palm-beach/) advertises immediate occupancy and remains the active developer-sales contact. That is a sales statement, not an independently inspected occupancy certificate or a claim that every residence is available. The documented Unit 1003 sale is an individual transaction only. Alba Reserve is unchanged.
- **OLIN Palm Beach:** [OKO Group’s portfolio](https://www.okogroup.com/portfolio/olin) listed **Pre-Construction Sales** and 32 units on September 23, 2026; the official [project site](https://olinpalmbeach.com/) names Corcoran Sunshine Marketing Group. The site displayed an inquiry form on direct review, but public copy does not depend on its continued visibility. The old inquiry-budget selections are not residence prices: every public price field requests current pricing, numeric Compare filters are blank, and no price band enters metadata or cards. Delivery remains unknown; a Palm Beach ZIP, a physical gallery, released plans, tour availability, and the old 41-unit figure remain unverified for public claims. The 32-versus-41 disagreement stays blocked from residence-count schema.

All primary inquiry links remain contextual to The Scott Gordon Group. Historical articles keep their dates, URLs and facts; the Alba market note already links to the current project page.
