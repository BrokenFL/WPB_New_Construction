# Olara — Copy Intelligence Brief

## Current Site Role

Olara is one of the core North Flagler waterfront pipeline projects and should be treated as a major lifestyle-and-amenity anchor for the corridor.

Existing inventory notes: `olara`; status `Under Construction`; address `1919 N Flagler Dr`; 26 stories; architecture by Arquitectonica; nearly 80,000 square feet of amenities; official sources include the project site, architecture page, floor plans, brochure, amenities brochure, and fact sheet.

## Official Positioning

The public positioning is centered on waterfront living, architecture, indoor-outdoor lifestyle, and a deep amenity program. The site/inventory language already emphasizes the 26-story Arquitectonica design and nearly 80,000 square feet of amenities.

## Buyer-Facing Translation

Olara should read as the amenities-forward North Flagler option: a large-scale, resort-like condominium designed to make the waterfront feel usable every day, not just visible from a balcony.

The buyer angle is not just luxury; it is convenience, breadth of amenities, and an active waterfront lifestyle in a building that can compete with resort/residential hybrids.

## Recommended Page Intro

Olara is the amenity-rich North Flagler waterfront project built around the idea that daily life should feel both residential and resort-like. Designed by Arquitectonica and planned with an unusually deep lifestyle program, the 26-story tower brings a large-scale luxury condominium experience to one of West Palm Beach’s most closely watched waterfront corridors.

## Signature Amenities / Lifestyle Buckets

- Wellness and fitness: position as a serious everyday wellness environment rather than a token gym.
- Waterfront leisure: emphasize pool, outdoor living, and water-facing lifestyle language where official sources support it.
- Social/private entertaining: frame lounges, dining, and gathering areas as extensions of the residence.
- Convenience/services: concierge-style support and building operations should be described only where official materials confirm them.

## Design / Architecture Notes

- Arquitectonica should be named in the page copy because it gives the building design credibility and connects it to South Florida high-rise architecture.
- The copy should avoid overclaiming views or final finishes; use "developer materials describe" for still-marketed concepts.
