# South Flagler House — Copy Intelligence Brief

## Current Site Role

South Flagler House is the flagship ultra-luxury South Flagler waterfront project and should be written with a different register than the North Flagler pages: more estate-like, architectural, and Palm Beach-adjacent.

Existing inventory notes: `south-flagler-house-north` and `south-flagler-house-south`; status `Under Construction`; address `1355 S Flagler Dr`; two 28-story buildings; split in the visualization model to preserve the twin-building silhouette.

## Official Positioning

The project is positioned around architectural permanence, waterfront luxury, and a two-tower composition facing Palm Beach Island. It should not read like generic downtown new construction.

## Buyer-Facing Translation

South Flagler House should be framed as a prestige waterfront address where architecture, scale, and Palm Beach proximity are the point. The buyer is likely comparing it less to amenity-heavy North Flagler projects and more to top-tier Palm Beach-adjacent ownership.

## Recommended Page Intro

South Flagler House is the architectural heavyweight of the South Flagler waterfront: a two-tower luxury condominium composition planned for 1355 South Flagler Drive, directly across the water from Palm Beach Island. Rather than selling only height or amenities, the project is positioned around permanence, formal design, and the rare feel of an estate-scale address in a vertical waterfront setting.
