## Bottom Line

The West Palm Beach Rosewood project appears to be the planned Rosewood Residences West Palm Beach at 2001 North Flagler Drive. It is a proposed Related Group and BH Group branded condominium tower on the North Flagler waterfront corridor, not yet a fully launched public sales site.

Use cautious status language: "planned", "proposed", "pending city approvals", and "pricing not yet formally released." Do not present floorplans, completion date, top-off date, or sales availability as confirmed.
