## Operating Rules

- Every project gets one tracker row.
- Every public page route must match the tracker route column.
- Every image must be either `authorized` or `pending authorization`.
- Every copied floorplan must live under its project folder in `public/projects/<project>/docs/floorplans/`.
- Compliance review material belongs in `05 Broker Compliance Review`.
- Buyer-facing PDFs/decks belong in `06 Buyer Packets`.
