- Homepage links to major corridors, project pages, Updates, Market Notes, and Inquiry.
- Corridor pages link to their related project pages.
- Project pages link to resources, floor plans, related updates, and Inquiry.
- Market Notes link to related buildings, related updates, and Inquiry.
- Updates link to related buildings or inquiry.
